title: Reading on screens, done properly date: 2026-10-02 tags: typography, reading excerpt:

Most of the reading I do happens on a phone, usually in short stretches. A page that respects that has a comfortable line length, enough space between lines, and text large enough that I never pinch to zoom.1

Line length

Around sixty to seventy characters per line is easy to follow. Longer lines make the eye lose its place on the way back; shorter lines break the rhythm of a sentence.

Line height

Body text wants more space between lines than headings do. Languages with stacked accents, like Vietnamese, need a little more again, or the marks collide with the line above.

Contrast

Near-black on white reads better than pure black, and dark mode looks calmer with off-white text. The difference is small, but over a long article it adds up.

Typography exists to honor content.
